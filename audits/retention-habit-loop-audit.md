# Retention and Habit Loop Audit

## Cadence

- Conditional

## Trigger conditions

- Onboarding, comeback loops, reminders, motivation, streaks, workout return behavior, weekly review loops, repeat-use nudges

## Required inputs

- Production behavior across first-session, repeat-session, lapse-recovery, and reminder touchpoints
- Current funnel context, retention hypotheses, and any same-run tickets from workflow, analytics, or UX audits
- User segments most at risk of bouncing, lapsing, or disengaging after setup

## Release-gating

- Yes when the shipped change materially affects activation, comeback confidence, or repeat-use health

## Deliverable

Every finding must include:

- Finding type
- Evidence from the actual retention loop, comeback flow, or repeat-use surface
- Confidence
- Affected user segments, motivation states, and touchpoints
- Why now
- Root cause or habit-loop gap
- Duplicate or related work check
- Ticket recommendation with owner hint and verification expectations

Audit focus:

- Whether the product creates a clear path from signup to first workout, second workout, and return after lapses
- Motivation, reassurance, cadence-building, and comeback guidance instead of shame or ambiguity
- Reminder timing, streak framing, weekly rituals, and other repeat-use mechanisms
