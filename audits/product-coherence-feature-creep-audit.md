# Product Coherence and Feature Creep Audit

## Cadence

- Conditional

## Trigger conditions

- Major scope expansion, new surfaces, overlapping product bets, complexity growth, audience-fit concerns, feature sprawl, or unclear core promise

## Required inputs

- Changed product surfaces, current product positioning, and notable same-run tickets from UX, backlog, monetization, and maintainability audits
- User-segment assumptions, onboarding expectations, and evidence for the product's core job-to-be-done
- Existing roadmap or backlog signals that indicate drift, duplication, or growing setup complexity

## Release-gating

- Advisory by default, but yes when product drift or complexity makes the current release hard to understand or safely operate

## Deliverable

Every finding must include:

- Finding type
- Evidence for product sprawl, overlapping surfaces, or unclear promise
- Confidence
- Affected routes, user segments, and decision points
- Why now
- Root cause or coherence gap
- Duplicate or related work check
- Ticket recommendation that narrows scope, clarifies hierarchy, or removes drift instead of adding more surface area

Audit focus:

- Whether the product still communicates one understandable core promise
- Complexity growth, fragmented workflows, and duplicate features that weaken product clarity
- Separation of concerns from UX, backlog, and monetization audits rather than re-litigating pricing inside this prompt
